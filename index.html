About Me

Born and raised in Belém do Pará, my curiosity for technology was inherited from my father, an early tech enthusiast who inspired me from a young age. After spending years building a dynamic career in Gastronomy, I decided to redirect my path back to my true passion: Software Development. The fast-paced kitchen environment taught me intense focus, agility, and adaptability under pressure — skills that I now bring directly into my code.

Currently based in Curitiba/PR (and open to relocation), I am pursuing a degree in Analysis and Systems Development (ADS) alongside practical certifications in Web Development and Database Administration from IFRS. I thrive on the visual creativity of Front-End development with CSS and JavaScript, watching ideas turn into vibrant, functional interfaces, while continuously building a solid foundation in Back-End to become a well-rounded Full Stack Developer.

I am deeply curious and relentlessly persistent — when facing a complex problem or project, I enter a state of total hyperfocus until a solution is found. Off-screen, you’ll find me sharpening my logical thinking with board games, hitting the pavement for road running, or training Jiu-Jitsu.
